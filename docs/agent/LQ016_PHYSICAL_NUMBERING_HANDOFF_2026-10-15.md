## Durable representation

`physical_item_numbering={version:1,basis,numbers:{rowId:positiveInteger},nextItemNo,initializedBy,initializedAt}` is server-owned. Existing numbers cannot change; deleted/reserved IDs remain in the ledger and new references advance its high-water mark. Transport row hints are validated and stripped from saved raw rows. Normal draft/proposal/application routes reserve numbering transactionally before accounting side effects. Abandoned/failed proposals can leave gaps; they never recycle references.
